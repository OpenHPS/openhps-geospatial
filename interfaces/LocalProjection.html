    Interface LocalProjection

    A local equirectangular projection centred on a WGS 84 origin.

    The projection is deliberately simple and deterministic: x is longitude scaled by the cosine of the origin latitude, y is latitude scaled by a constant. It is invertible, so a connection point computed on the metre grid can be converted back to a geographical position.
